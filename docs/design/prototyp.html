Layout

The dice tray never gets narrower than 500px. Columns are added only while it keeps that width and the scorecard needs no sideways scroll; otherwise the scorecard drops below the dice, on any device. Players and the game log get a column last, and slide in from the right under “Players” when there is no room. More players make the scorecard wider, so eight players drop to fewer columns sooner.

Style principles

  • The felt is the heroThe dice tray is the only dark, saturated object. Everything around it stays light and calm.
  • Readable across the tableSeveral people read the scores at once. Figures are large, heavy and tabular.
  • One action colourViolet means “do something”. Pink is for secondary actions, never the main one.
  • A player keeps their colourScorecard dot, avatar, log entry. Eight colours for the eight-player limit.
